On September 9, 2026, the guild opened the semester with Untangled, its orientation for A.Y. 2026 to 2027, at IC's Bar and Cafe in Los Baños.

The night was themed after Tangled. Members and guests spent it untangling a world of data together, turning messy nodes into insights and making new connections across the guild.
